Phalleon Gallery — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

About the Application

Phalleon Gallery is an owner-operated application intended to publish and organize original artwork for Phalleon gaming communities on Pinterest.

Information Accessed

When authorized, the application may access the connected Pinterest account's boards, Pins, and account identifiers necessary for publishing.

How Information Is Used

The application uses authorized Pinterest access to publish approved illustrations, organize Pins by gaming community, and retrieve Pin information for Discord announcements.

Data Sharing

The application is not intended to sell personal information or provide Pinterest account access to unrelated third parties. Published artwork and its associated information may be publicly visible on Pinterest and shared through Discord.

Security and Revocation

Pinterest authorization will use the official Pinterest API. The account owner can revoke application authorization through Pinterest.

Contact

Privacy inquiries may be directed to the Phalleon operator through the official community contact channel.
